Privacy Policy & Support

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: Linogram collects no data at all. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app never connects to the internet.

Who we are

Linogram is developed by Raw Coder, Romania. For any question, email rawcoder@icloud.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress (which puzzles you solved, the board of a puzzle you left halfway) and your settings (sound, haptics, assisted mode) are stored only on your device so the game works. They are never sent anywhere and are deleted when you remove the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Linogram has no network code, no third-party SDKs, no advertising and no analytics. We do not know who plays it.

Children

Linogram is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add optional statistics in a future version), we will update this page and the date above before the new version is released.

Support

Stuck on a puzzle? Turn on Assisted mode in the ⋯ menu: the game highlights a line you can solve and explains why. You can replay the six lessons any time from How to play on the home screen.

Found a bug or have an idea? Email rawcoder@icloud.com, we read everything.
